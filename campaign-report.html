The Problem

Brands spend lakhs on creators and get engagement back, not revenue.

Brands run influencer marketing campaigns and get views, likes and comments back — not proof that anything sold. They spend lakhs on creators, the content performs, and then the funnel just stops. There is no single method to say whether the campaign actually worked.

Without that proof, every renewal conversation starts from zero. The brand has to take it on faith that the spend was worth it.

  • Brands see: views, likes, comments — engagement with no revenue attached.
  • Brands want: how much did this creator, this post, this campaign actually sell?
  • The gap: no single source of truth connects content to a completed sale.

The Insight

LehLah already sits where content meets a purchase.

LehLah already sits at the point where a creator's content meets a purchase — through Auto-DM for creators on the platform, and through affiliate links for creators who aren't. We already had the raw signal to close this gap. It just needed to become a first-class feature a brand could open and trust: Campaign Report.

Solution: Two Paths, One Report

It had to work whether or not the creator was already on LehLah.

A campaign report had to work whether or not the creator was already on LehLah, so we built two attribution paths that land in the same place.

  • LehLah creator: uses Auto-DM and tags the product in their content — sales are tracked automatically.
  • Non-LehLah creator: gets a generated affiliate link for the product to use in their story or reel — sales attributed to that link.
  • Brand view: both paths roll into one full-funnel report — what the creator actually drove, not just what they posted.

Retailer-Level Attribution

Sales don't happen in one place.

A brand's campaign can drive purchases on a marketplace as easily as on their own site, so the report breaks sales down by retailer instead of assuming a single storefront.

Myntra Flipkart Meesho Amazon Shopsy Nykaa Tira Ajio

D2C, Integrated in Under 2 Minutes

Marketplaces cover most of a brand's sales, but not all of it.

The LehLah Shopify app connects a brand's own D2C storefront to the same report in under two minutes, so direct-website sales sit alongside marketplace sales instead of living in a separate spreadsheet.

  • Install: brand installs the LehLah Shopify app on their store.
  • Connect: the app links storefront orders to the campaign automatically — no manual export.
  • Report: D2C sales appear in the same Campaign Report as every marketplace.

Impact

Campaign Report turns "we posted some content and got some views" into a number a brand can put in a renewal deck: this creator, on this campaign, drove this many sales, on these retailers. That's the difference between influencer marketing bought as media and influencer marketing run as a growth channel with a payback period.

What I Owned

Defined the attribution model across LehLah and affiliate creators, designed the full-funnel report structure end-to-end, and worked with Engineering to wire up retailer- and D2C-level tracking.
