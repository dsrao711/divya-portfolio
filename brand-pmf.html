The Problem

A post full of views and engagement doesn't hand a brand any revenue.

Running an influencer campaign looks straightforward from the outside: find creators who match the brand's aesthetic, brief them, get the content live. The hard part is what happens after the content goes live — brands care about revenue, and a post full of views and engagement doesn't hand them any.

The honest fallback is to spend more: run the same content as paid ads to force a sale out of it. That works, but it means the organic creator spend bought attention, not revenue — the two budgets never talk to each other.

  • Step 1: find creators matching the brand's aesthetic.
  • Step 2: brief them, get content live.
  • What brands actually want: proof the content sold something.
  • The fallback: pay again, in ads, to manufacture the sale the organic post didn't prove.

The Insight

We had the answer sitting in our own data and didn't know it yet.

LehLah has 13K+ monthly active creators across geographies making content and driving real sales through affiliate links — for brands who had never run a single campaign with us.

Take Mamaearth: a popular D2C brand that LehLah creators were very likely already generating sales for, organically, through affiliate content Mamaearth never briefed and never paid for.

Solution: LehLah SaaS Creator Discovery

It shows a brand exactly who's already selling for them.

LehLah SaaS bridges that gap. It shows a brand exactly which LehLah creators are already driving sales for them, organically, on the LehLah app — before any campaign, any brief, any spend.

  • What this means: the creator is already endorsing the brand for free.
  • And: they've already driven a real, attributed sale — not just a like or a follow.
  • What the brand gets: visibility into these creators, the ability to launch a campaign, connect directly, and track sales going forward.
  • What the creator gets: a direct paid opportunity with a brand their own audience was already buying.

Why This Beats Searching for Creators Cold

Organic sales is a far stronger signal than any filter.

Every creator marketplace can filter by category, follower count or aesthetic. None of that tells a brand who actually converts. Organic, unprompted sales is a far stronger signal than any filter, because it isn't a guess about fit — it already happened.

That's the product-market fit this feature finds: not "which creators look right for this brand", but "which creators have already proven their audience buys this brand."

Proof: HUL and Marico

LehLah creators had organically generated lakhs of rupees in sales for brands like HUL and Marico — unknowingly, on both sides. Once that surfaced through LehLah SaaS, it changed the relationship: both brands now run regular campaigns with LehLah, and use the discovery tool as their primary way of finding creators worth paying.

Impact

This turned an invisible, unmonetised organic effect into a renewal engine: brands didn't need to be sold on influencer marketing in the abstract — they were shown the specific creators and the specific rupees already flowing, then asked if they wanted more of it, faster and on purpose.
